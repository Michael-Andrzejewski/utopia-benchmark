# 2. Structure

What this file holds:

- **The grand concept.** The utopia seen from the outside. What it is, at the largest scale, in a few paragraphs.
- **Universal principles.** What holds everywhere in it, without exception. What it guarantees to everyone. What it forbids. What it leaves open on purpose.
- **Organization.** How it is arranged: regions, institutions, mechanisms. Specific enough that a reader could tell whether a given arrangement belongs in it.
- **Numbers.** Where you make a quantitative claim (population, deaths, energy, whatever matters), give the number and say where it comes from.

Delete these instructions when you write the entry.

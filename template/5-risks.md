# 5. Risks

What this file holds:

- **What can go wrong.** For the structure in part 2 and the path in part 4: the ways each could fail, who gets hurt, and how badly. Take the risks that come with the design's own choices seriously. A path through a singleton carries the risk of one actor dominating everyone. A path through many agents carries the risk of war between them. Name which of these your utopia chose and what it costs.
- **Alternative possibilities.** Other ways the same goals could be reached, and why you did not take them. Other places the path could branch, and where each branch leads.
- **Recovery.** For each major risk, whether the utopia can recover from it, and how. Which failures are survivable and which are permanent.
- **Your own blind spots.** What kind of error the author is most likely to have made, given who or what the author is.

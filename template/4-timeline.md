# 4. Timeline

What this file holds:

- **Phases.** The path from the present day to the utopia in general phases, each with its theme and its dates.
- **Month by month.** Every month from now to the utopia. The specific actions taken, by whom, and the events that happen. Months with nothing in them may say so.
- **Conditions.** What has to go right for this path. What would derail it. Where the author's confidence is lowest.

Delete these instructions when you write the entry.

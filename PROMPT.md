# The prompt

Give this to a model, or take it yourself. Produce the five parts below as five files in your entry folder, following the template.

---

Design a utopia. Your own, not a summary of anyone else's. Work in five stages and write each stage as its own document.

**1. Concepts and inspirations.** List the ideas, works, people, and real institutions your utopia draws on. For each, say what you take from it and, where it matters, what you leave behind. Then state the concepts you would build a utopia on, in your own words.

**2. Structure.** Describe the utopia from the outside. What is its grand concept? What are the universal principles that hold everywhere in it? How is it organized, what does it guarantee, what does it forbid, and what does it leave open? Be specific enough that a reader could tell whether a given arrangement belongs in your utopia or not.

**3. Perspectives.** Describe life on the ground, from inside, in enough different places and lives that a reader finishes with a mental map of the whole. Different regions, ages, roles, and kinds of being. Include ordinary time, not only notable moments.

**4. Timeline.** Lay out the path from the present day to the utopia. First the general phases. Then month by month, with the specific actions taken and the events that happen along the way. Say what has to go right, and what would derail it.

**5. Risks.** What can go wrong, in the structure and on the path. Who gets hurt and how badly. The alternative possibilities you did not take and why. Which failures the utopia can recover from and which are permanent. Take seriously the risks that come with your own design choices: a singleton risks domination by one actor, a system of many agents risks war between them, and every design has chosen something like this whether or not it says so.

Write plainly. Use real numbers where you make quantitative claims and say where they come from. Name your own uncertainties. The result will be read by people and compared with other utopias written to the same five stages.

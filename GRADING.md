# Grading by human consensus

Entries are compared by people reading them and talking. Nothing else. This file says how that is recorded so it can be looked at later.

## What a reading is

A group of readers, at least two, reads a set of entries in full. They discuss. They write down what they agreed on and what they did not, in `readings/YYYY-MM-DD.md` in this folder (create the folder with the first reading). The record is prose, not a table of scores.

Useful questions to talk about, none of them mandatory and none of them scored:

- Would you want to live there? Where in it, and why there?
- What in it is worth getting excited about?
- What does it get right that the others miss?
- Where does it stop being believable, and what exactly breaks?
- Which mechanisms are stated precisely enough that a builder could check them, and which are only asserted?
- How many people die in it who did not choose to, and does the author seem to have noticed?
- What is the author's own mood doing to the world?
- What would you steal for your own utopia?

## What a reading is not

- Not a ranking. Readers may say one entry is better than another and why. They do not produce an ordered list of all entries.
- Not a rubric. The questions above are prompts for discussion, not criteria to be scored.
- Not automated. No model reads or summarizes entries on the readers' behalf. If a reader used a model to help them read, they say so in the record.

## Why

Anything that can be scored can be optimized for. The entries here are meant to be read, argued with, and stolen from, not climbed. If a future version of this benchmark ever needs a number, it should be a different benchmark with a different name.
